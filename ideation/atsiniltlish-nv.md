# Atsiniltł'ish I.nída'iiníltsohígíí, Doo Bilagáana Silį́į́' Da

* — "The telescope, not the television." A short Diné bizaad essay with
English glosses. Wave 73, SuperInstance. Written simply and with respect;
Diné grammar organizes around verbs and process, which is exactly the
right grammar for this thesis.*

## T'óó ajidléigo (The distinction, plainly)

Bilagáana (television) éí t'áá łahgo naaltsoos jiníigo át'é — t'áá
hólǫ́ǫgo naaltsoos nít'éego doo naʼáníígóó. Atsiniltł'ish (telescope)
éí t'áá hwinahgo hólǫ́: ní tsíída ayóo ákót'éego ní nihookáá'.
Ákót'éego t'áá jí át'éego éí t'áá binahjį' nitł'izgo naaldeeh —
*who supplies the judgment.*

T'áá hwééhgo naaltsoos nitł'iz bitł'óól binaa'ni' (artificial
intelligence) éí bilagáana át'é. Diné bizaadji' nímasi' — superimposed
intelligence éí t'áá nííká hadiit'ąągo át'é: iiná doo hane' biká'
na'ahaidiilná, áádóó bee haz'ą́ągo na'íídílá. Nitsáhákees nináldzád —
*it does not conclude; it lines up the evidence and the person walks the
meaning.*

## Oodláád (Building blocks, made today)

Doo t'áá hó-dahbąąnígę́ę́' da — technology éí dį́į́' béeso bits'ą́ą́dę́ę́
néesa, áádóó óolyé. T'áá nída'iiníltsohígíí éí t'áá hóchsą' go
naaghá. K'ad shį́į́ naaltsoos naaltsoos doo yitł'įhgo bee njı́łdzih
(receipts), doo yeedła'ji' hólne' (diffs), áádóó ch'ééh jiją́ągo
binaalyéhé (small models with narrow charters) — díí éí 2036 béégashii
bina'ada' *genome* át'é. T'áá dei'níłtłiz — *replayed, audited, bred*.

## Hózhóóji' naniná (Tissue, not tower)

Jádi' dąągo naalnį́į́hgo (cells iterating) t'áá áłah nitsáhákees
naakai — tissue. Ntsékees éí k'é; nanit'ą́ą' éí bee haz'ą́ąniiłńį́
(young cell earning reach); áádóó béésh *na'ídíkáh* (ledger) éí
tsin naa'eeł át'é — *the ledger is the scaffolding of the bones.*
Weight éí naaki yą́ą́h — t'óó ahayóígo (soft), nitsáhákeesgo
(reflective), áádóó t'áá hóchxǫ́ǫgo (reflexive). T'áá hólǫ́ǫgo éí
bíla'ashdla'ii kar doo nidoolchį́h da — *at every moment there is a
state that can be saved, at a resolution chosen wisely, fine where the
future is precious, coarse where forgetting is cheap.*

## K'é (Small information, federated)

T'áá ntsxį́į́ go hólne' (small data) éí náás ákó dzidzé — *compounds* —
doohandi ch'ééh naaltsoos (ledger) biyi' t'óó ahónéélé t'éiyá.
Bįįh yaa áłchį́į́hgo (the moth) éí njchxǫ́ǫgo hólǫ́ — *true randomness,
honest dice* — díí éí chidí bina'ada' áADI daats'í jíní́.

## Nááshee łtso (The opcode, tonight)

T'áá hó-ajidá -- file-cell, standing instruction; poke; receipt
`{parent_hash, delta, seed, ts}`; t'áá lágo ayóo ádáhojílką́ą́
(one-sentence self-amendment). Naakiigo ch'ééh -- tissue of two.
Táá'go k'éhodoołką́ą́ -- ripple. *Everything larger is the same tick,
repeated.* T'áá shǫ́ǫhgo bee hózhǫ́ -- *the first tick is already small
enough to afford, and the receipt keeps the walk beautiful.*

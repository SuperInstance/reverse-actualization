# The Optimism Ledger — seed (wave-73b)

The framing existed from the first walk: "every step back carrying one sentence of
optimism." A framing is not an artifact. This is the artifact: one receipted sentence of
optimism per rung, per walker, with a canonical selection per rung. Seeded from the
NEGATIVE ladder (rungs −1…−10, `wave73b-LADDER-NEGATIVE.md`); the positive rungs (2036 →
tonight) are pending their own pass with the same discipline.

The rule that makes it a ledger and not a mood board: **each sentence must cite the
receipt that earns it** — the mechanism, the primitive, or the measured number that makes
the step "always going to be possible." Optimism is cheap when the log is clean.

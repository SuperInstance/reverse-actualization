## PART A — Wide-internet scout (26 leads)

### Thread 1 — Drosophila connectome & circuit motifs (6 leads)

1. **FlyWire connectome (Nature 2024)** — https://www.nature.com/immersive/d42859-024-00053-4/index.html — The complete adult-fly-brain wiring diagram (~140k neurons, ~50M synapses). This is *why* the fly is the fruit-fly of neuroscience: every motif below is an actual readable graph, making fly motifs the only learning motifs we can lift at mechanism level with full wiring confidence.
2. **Diversity of visual inputs to Kenyon cells** (Nat Commun 2024) — https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-49616-z — Kenyon cells (KBs) are a random high-dimensional fan-out of sensory input, kept sparse; inputs turn out multimodal. Mechanism-level: random projection + winner threshold is implementable in a handful of cells.
3. **Presynaptic developmental plasticity allows robust sparse coding** (eLife 2020) — https://elifesciences.org/articles/52278 — How the sparse code is *maintained*: local presynaptic normalization keeps a fixed winning fraction. A teacherless stabilization loop — the mechanism our `resistance` field would need.
4. **Distinct circuit motifs evaluate opposing innate values of odors** (Cell 2025) — https://www.cell.com/cell/fulltext/S0092-8674(25)01021-9 — Lateral-horn motifs are hardwired valuation circuits requiring no experience. Precedent for splitting a substrate into innate motif (wiring) + learned overlay (mushroom body) — exactly an "innate defaults, receipt-earned overrides" design.
5. **Learning with reward prediction errors in a model of the mushroom body** (Nat Commun 2021) — https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-22592-4 — Dopamine neurons broadcast a reward-prediction-error scalar that gates a purely local synaptic rule. The fly does TD-learning with no backprop and no optimizer; this is the cleanest 3-factor rule to port: local pre/post + one broadcast surprise.
6. **Can a Fruit Fly Learn Word Embeddings?** (arXiv 2021) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.06887 — Formalizes the MB motif (random expansion, k-winner sparsity) as an algorithm competitive with LSH. Proof that a fly motif is a *real algorithm*, not metaphor — the founding paper for fly-as-tiny-algorithm.

**What makes the fly the fruit-fly:** complete wiring (FlyWire), few identifiable neurons per motif, behavioral readout is cheap, and the motifs decompose (expansion → sparsity → valuation → dopaminergic write) — each stage implementable alone. All four stages above are mechanism-level.

### Thread 2 — Minimal-learning organisms & substrates (6 leads)

7. **Habituation sans neurons in a unicellular organism** (Current Biology 2022) — https://www.cell.com/current-biology/fulltext/S0960-9822(22)01908-X — Single ciliates decrement their response like habituating animals, with no synapses. The minimal model organism at OUR scale: learning as a per-cell field update, literally.
8. **Biochemically plausible models of habituation for single-cell learning** (Current Biology 2024) — https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0960982224014301 — Minimal intracellular rule families (resource depletion / channel inactivation) that reproduce habituation in one cell. Direct candidate spec-lines for a per-cell resistance update.
9. **Learning in Aplysia: synaptic plasticity from both sides** (Trends Neurosci 2004) — https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0166223603003333 — Identified-synapse habituation/sensitization with a two-timescale split: short-term (local, reversible, covalent) vs long-term (nuclear, consolidation). Biological template for fast-lane vs committed-lane learning.
10. **The Self-Tuning Neuron: synaptic scaling** (Cell 2008, Turrigiano) — https://www.cell.com/fulltext/S0092-8674(08)01298-1 — Each neuron tracks its own activity and rescales to stay in band, protecting learned structure. The job description for a homeostatic governor field.
11. **Behavioral plasticity, learning, and memory in C. elegans** (Curr Opin Neurobiol) — https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S095943881200147X — Associative learning, habituation, and relearning in 302 neurons with a fully known connectome: existence proof that one mapped micro-network carries multiple learning modes.
12. **Physarum intelligent foraging survey** (arXiv 2021) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.00172 — Slime-mold transport rule (tube thickening ∝ flux) solves routing/adaptation with zero gradients and zero labels; companion review: arXiv 1712.02910. The ultimate "learning as local flow reallocation" precedent.

### Thread 3 — Tiny/iterative model systems (5 leads)

13. **Towards Understanding Mixture of Experts** (arXiv 2022) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.02813 — When sparse conditional routing to small specialists beats one big model, and how. Cell graphs with split/router cells are structurally MoE; Kenyon layers are biological MoE. (HF explainer companion: huggingface.co/blog/moe.)
14. **Test-Time Training with Self-Supervision** (Sun et al.) — https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.13231 — Learning at inference on the data actually arriving. A ledger-based cell system is *always* test-time: every replay is a training opportunity. (Gap note: the "federated with tiny per-node data" search died at the scraper level; TTT + tinyML carry the thread.)
15. **Continuous Self-Improvement via Test-time Training with Verifier-Driven Self-Labeling** (arXiv 2025) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.19475 — Iterative improvement on tiny self-generated data, gated by a verifier. Rhymes exactly with our judge-gate/seal protocol: only verdict-passed deltas commit.
16. **Learning sparsity in reservoir computing through a bio-inspired algorithm** (arXiv 2019) — https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.08600 — Reservoirs train only a tiny readout; this adds fly-like sparsity learning. Bridges thread 1 → thread 3: few nodes, local state, echo-state property = order-friendly memory. Companion: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Echo_state_network.
17. **HyperNetworks** (Ha, Dai, Le 2016) — https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.09106 — A small network generates the weights of another. For a cell system: a few 'program cells' could emit per-cell parameters (micro-policies) — parameter generation instead of parameter storage, keeping each cell's learned state tiny.

### Thread 4 — "Mechanical learning" precedents (6 leads)

18. **The Forward-Forward Algorithm** (Hinton 2022) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.13345 — Two forward passes replace backprop; each layer learns from local "goodness" comparisons. Flagship proof that learning can be *mechanical*: no backward pass, no global optimizer, tiny per-layer state.
19. **A theory for the sparsity emerged in the Forward-Forward algorithm** (arXiv 2023) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.05667 — FF self-organizes sparse representations for free — the analytic bridge from Kenyon sparsity to no-backprop rules.
20. **Predictive Coding Approximates Backprop along Arbitrary Computation Graphs** (Salvatori, Millidge et al.) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.04182 — Purely local prediction-error updates provably approximate backprop. License: local rules are not a compromise, they can compute the same thing. (Lightweight variant: "Predictive Coding Light", Nat Commun 2025, s41467-025-64234-z.)
21. **A Learning Theory for Reward-Modulated STDP** (Izhikevich 2007) — https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1000180 — Dopamine broadcasts one scalar; local STDP does the rest. The canonical 3-factor rule; the fly's dopamine-RPE and any receipt-valuation we design both live here. (Digital-synapse companion: ieee 10489426.)
22. **Oja's plasticity rule under biological constraints** (arXiv 2024) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.08408 — Hebb + self-limiting term using only pre/post activity; streaming PCA with no gradients or labels. Companion: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oja's_rule. The minimal mathematical core of a per-cell learning rule.
23. **Differentiable Logic Cellular Automata** (arXiv 2025, Google self-organising-systems) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.04912 — Learning *inside* a cellular automaton: local rule tables on discrete neighborhoods. Companion classic: distill.pub/2020/growing-ca/ (Growing Neural Cellular Automata). Closest existing prior art to learning on our grid.

### Thread 5 — Data-for-the-user (not for the cloud) (3 leads)

24. **Understanding, Protecting, and Augmenting Human Cognition with AI** (arXiv 2025) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.21036 — "Tools for thought": AI as an instrument augmenting a person's own processing, not media replacing it — the formal version of "telescope not television".
25. **Alan Kay, A Personal Computer for Children of All Ages** (1972) — https://andymatuschak.org/files/papers/Kay%20-%201972%20-%20A%20Personal%20Computer%20for%20Children%20of%20All%20Ages.pdf — The Dynabook charter: the machine is a personal instrument for one person's own material. Normative target: the ledger belongs to the user, on their side of the glass.
26. **Tiny Machine Learning: Progress and Futures** (arXiv 2024) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.19076 — Learning on microcontrollers with kilobyte budgets proves "learning on the user's device with the user's data" is deployable engineering, not aspiration. Companion: arXiv 2204.00827. (Industry flywheel framing: NVIDIA glossary "data flywheel", github.com/NVIDIA-AI-Blueprints/data-flywheel.)

## PART C — SPEC DRAFT: "Mechanical learning" for the quilt cell system

A learning-rule family is **mechanical learning** iff it satisfies all of the following. Each line is bound to its strongest scouting + local evidence.

1. **LOCALITY** — a cell updates only from its own four fields (`potential, resistance, entropy, is_split`) and reads of immediate neighbors' public fields; no backward pass, no global optimizer state, no epoch loop. [Oja 2408.08408; Forward-Forward 2212.13345; growing-CA distill.pub/2020/growing-ca/; local: original_draft.mojo:18-21]
2. **ORDER-INDEPENDENCE (parallel-first)** — the accumulated state must be the fold of an associative-commutative update over the receipt stream, so any replay order or any replica converges to the same learned state; updates execute concurrently and reconcile like rec-diffs. [jev-quilt SUBSTRATE.md:60-69 list-homomorphism chain; reconciliation.mjs P8; local refimpl project() is pure per-cell]
3. **RECEIPTS, NOT GRADIENTS** — every learning update emits a deterministic, hashable ledger delta (CellDiff/receipt); "weights" are the folded ledger, "training" is replay, "rollback" is refusing a delta — fail-closed, not fail-different. [DEEP-STUDY-71:65-93 (248==248 replay); r-STDP pcbi.1000180 replaces gradients with gated local updates; verifier-gated TTT 2505.19475]
4. **SPARSITY-PROTECTED CREDIT (the fly motif)** — credit assignment uses random fan-out + k-winner dilution: `is_split` is the expansion event, high neighbor `resistance` is the APL/lateral-inhibition analog, so each update touches few cells and each cell's ledger stays tiny. [eLife 52278; KC diversity s41467-024-49616-z; fly-embeddings 2101.06887; FF-sparsity 2311.05667; local: exoj selflocal.mjs:38-41 "rank, never collapse"]
5. **HOMEOSTATIC GOVERNOR** — a slow, local stabilizer keeps each cell in an activity band: learning may only raise `potential` when the cell's own `entropy` is in band; `resistance` rescales accumulated structure. Habituation = monotone resistance growth under repeated same-value stimuli. [Turrigiano Cell 2008; unicellular habituation S0960-9822(22)01908-X + biochemical models S0960982224014301; local: exoj soft_write README.md:274-279]
6. **LOCAL SURPRISE AS THIRD FACTOR** — the dopamine/RPE slot is filled by a measurable field quantity (entropy change or divergence from the receipt-forecast of a neighbor's value), broadcasting within one neighborhood only; the 3rd factor must be computable from fields the cell already holds. [MB dopamine RPE s41467-021-22592-4; r-STDP pcbi.1000180; predictive-coding error locality 2006.04182]
7. **TWO TIME SCALES** — fast lane: potential deltas, reversible, uncommitted; slow lane: resistance/ledger commitment only via sealed receipts. Consolidation is a job for the seal/rehydration machinery, not the cell. [Aplysia S0166223603003333 short-vs-long term; local: REHYDRATION-72 decay curve + scheduler]
8. **TINY-DATA GRACE (monotone-or-null)** — with n examples the rule must improve a pre-registered readout or do nothing — never diverge — and its behavior under data loss must be measured and schedulable (the decay curve d(t) is part of the rule's spec sheet). [quilt-jepa G/R1 learning-sanity gates registration-v3.json:12,22 + plasticity-cost law v7; Physarum: useful adaptation from zero-label local rules; tinyML 2403.19076]
9. **TEST-TIME, USER-SIDE PLASTICITY** — there is no separate training phase: the substrate adapts continuously to the stream in front of it (the user's own data, on the user's side of the glass), and no cell's state requires centralized data. [TTT 1909.13231; Kay 1972; tools-for-thought 2508.21036; local: decomposition flywheel comparison.md:46, murmur trust writeback MESH.md:36]
10. **FALSIFIABLE BY CONSTRUCTION** — each rule variant ships with a pre-registered prediction and sealed receipts, and progresses by KILL/KEEP verdicts the way genetics progresses by fly crosses. [cell-lab rounds 1-4 preregistration; quilt-jepa registration-*.json; atlas GRAPH-R2-72.md:42]

**Three-line compression (the elevator spec):**
> Mechanical learning = per-cell updates computable from the four fields + neighbor reads (locality), folded order-independently over a receipt ledger so replicas converge (receipts-not-gradients, parallel-first), with sparsity protecting credit (fly motif), a homeostatic resistance governor (habituation/scaling), and a sealed two-timescale commitment policy that degrades gracefully on tiny data (monotone-or-null, measured decay).
